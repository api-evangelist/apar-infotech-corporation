Beach clean-up activity with Singapore Beach Warriors Marine litter is a growing global challenge that requires collective and comprehensive action. As a part of Apar Technologies’ CSR initiatives, a beach cleanup activity was conducted by a few enthusiasts from our Singapore office, at Pasir Ris beach area 1, on 2nd July 2022. This was in association with the Singapore Beach Warriors, a team that hosts coastal cleanups in Singapore to create an awareness on the importance of taking steps to reduce trash buildup that is endangering our marine life.
