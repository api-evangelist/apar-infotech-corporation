Cap Donation Drive Along with the volunteers at an organization that works with dormitory workers in Singapore, Apar conducted a cap donation drive. The caps were distributed at Terusan Recreation Centre, Jalan Pan, Westlite Dorm, and Keintech Dorm. These workers work in scorching heat for long hours and our small help was gladly received by them.
